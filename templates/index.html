About Our Project

Our Traffic Analysis Platform leverages advanced computer vision and machine learning to provide real-time insights into traffic conditions. Powered by the YOLO model, it detects and tracks vehicles, calculates lane densities, and visualizes congestion through intuitive heatmaps.

Key Features:

  • Real-time vehicle detection and tracking
  • Dynamic traffic light simulation based on congestion
  • Congestion heatmaps (Green: Low, Red: High)
  • Detailed metrics on vehicle count, density, and speed
